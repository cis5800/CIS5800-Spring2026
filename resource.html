Textbooks (Optional)

The following references are recommended companions to the course. Both are freely available online.

Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision

Richard Hartley & Andrew Zisserman — 2nd Edition, Cambridge University Press

Core Reference

Covers projective geometry, camera models, epipolar geometry, structure from motion, and more. The definitive reference for geometric computer vision.

Elements of Geometry for Computer Vision and Computer Graphics

Tomas Pajdla — Lecture Notes, 2021

Free PDF

Concise treatment of projective and algebraic geometry foundations underlying camera models and multi-view reconstruction.
